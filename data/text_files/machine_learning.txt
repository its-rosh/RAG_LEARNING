Machine learning is a branch of artificial intelligence that enables computers to learn patterns from data and 
    make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed for every task. 
    A typical machine learning workflow includes collecting data, cleaning and preprocessing it, selecting useful features, training a model, and evaluating its performance. 
    Common learning approaches include supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and reinforcement learning. 
    Algorithms such as linear regression, decision trees, support vector machines, and neural networks are widely used for different problems. 
    The quality of the training data strongly affects model performance. 
    Machine learning is applied in recommendation systems, fraud detection, image recognition, forecasting, healthcare, and natural language processing.
